Dearest Rani,

World Letter Writing Day

Today is Letter Writing Day, and when I thought about who deserves every single word of my heart, you were the only one on my mind.

They say distance tests love, but with you, it has only shown me how limitless love can be. Even with all the miles, time zones, and screens between us, you feel closer to me than anything else in this world. You are in the quiet moments of my morning, in every melody I hear throughout the day, and in my last thought before I fall asleep.

"Distance is just a test to see how far love can travel."

I miss your smile, the sound of your real laughter, and the simple warmth of your presence. But knowing that you are out there—loving me, supporting me, and sharing your heart with me—makes every single mile worth enduring.

This distance is only temporary, but what we have is forever. I count down the days until I don’t have to write these feelings down on paper or send them across a phone screen, but can finally whisper them to you in person.

Thank you for being my anchor, my favorite thought, and my queen. No matter how far apart we are today, my heart is right there beside you.

Happy Letter Day, my love.

Forever and always yours across every mile,

Your Love, Rahad ❤️
